# 0003: Species scope is order Serpentes

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-23

## Decision

The species registry takes all snakes (order Serpentes) from the Reptile Database: 4,249 species in 33 families, venomous or not.

## Why

All snakes, not only venomous ones: the device must tell a harmless snake from a dangerous one, and protecting harmless snakes from being killed out of fear is part of the charter.
