# 0001: VITA-SHIELD is a sovereign digital commons

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-23

## Decision

VITA-SHIELD is held in stewardship as a digital commons. No patents. All work published openly. Contributors keep sovereignty over their own data.

## Why

The problem is global and shared. A single owner would create the silos this project exists to end, and patents would slow the spread of whatever works.

## Open questions

- Licences for code and canonical data (to be settled once source licences are known).
- Long-term steward: CSDC now; a neutral home (foundation or multilateral body) later.
