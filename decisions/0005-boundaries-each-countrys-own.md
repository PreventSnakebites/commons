# 0005: Regions use each country's own published boundaries

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-23

## Decision

Regions (countries, states and districts) come from each country's own published boundaries, through geoBoundaries gbOpen. No single global composite is used.

## Why

The commons serves the whole family and takes no side in any boundary claim. A global composite must choose one line wherever borders are disputed. Using each country's own boundaries means that where two countries claim the same land, both claims are recorded as published, and a snake living there appears under every country that claims it. No one searching for the snakes of their place misses one because of politics.

## Rules

- Each boundary keeps its own licence, source and year, as published.
- The commons never redraws, merges or adjudicates a boundary.
